# Saved Places (Your Places)

module: saved_places

## Overview

Q: What are Saved Places? A: Saved Places lets riders store addresses they use often.

## Add New Shortcut

Q: What is "Add New" on the home screen? A: It is a shortcut to select pickup/drop-off addresses faster.

## Managing Places

Q: What can be done inside Saved Places? A: Places can be pinned, edited, deleted, and favorited.
