# Requesting a Ride

module: ride_request

## Basics

Q: How do I request a ride? A: Choose pickup and destination, choose a pricing mode, apply customization if needed, then get matched to a driver.

## Tracking and Trip Lifecycle

Q: Can I track my driver in real time? A: Yes. Moto supports real-time tracking.

Q: Can I rate the driver after the trip? A: Yes. Rating the driver is supported.
