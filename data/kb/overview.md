# Moto Overview

module: overview

## What is Moto

Q: What is Moto? A: Moto is a ride-hailing app that helps riders request rides, choose pricing modes, and helps drivers receive ride orders.

Q: What does Moto do in simple terms? A: Moto lets riders choose pickup and destination, choose a pricing mode, customize the ride, get matched, track the trip, and rate the driver.

## Availability

Q: Is Moto live right now? A: Moto is in pre-launch.

Q: Where is Moto launching? A: Moto plans to launch in Addis Ababa, starting with the Bole area, then expanding.

Q: When is Moto launching? A: Moto has not announced a public launch date yet.

Q: Is Moto available outside Addis Ababa? A: This is not documented in the current Moto KB v1.

## Main Difference

Q: What is Moto's main difference vs other ride apps? A: Moto's main difference is the pricing system: Instant for speed, Flex for control, and Auto for a calm time-based price-clearing process.
