# Pricing Modes

module: pricing_modes

## Overview

Q: Why does Moto have three pricing modes? A: The three modes help riders choose what fits the moment: speed (Instant), control (Flex), or a calm price process (Auto).

Q: Which pricing mode is fastest? A: Instant is designed for speed.

Q: Which mode gives the most control? A: Flex gives control through negotiation.

Q: Which mode is calm and automatic? A: Auto is a calm, time-based price-clearing process.

## Instant Mode

Q: What is Instant? A: Instant auto-assigns a driver quickly.

Q: What happens after I choose Instant? A: The system assigns a driver automatically.

Q: Can I select a driver's preferred language? A: In some cities, yes. This is limited and applies to Instant ride orders only.

## Flex Mode

Q: What is Flex? A: Flex lets riders negotiate fare with drivers. Riders can accept, counter, or decline.

Q: What actions are available in Flex? A: Accept, counter, or decline.

## Auto Mode (Canonical Definition)

Q: What is Auto? A: Moto Auto is a calm, time-based price-clearing process. It runs for 30–45 seconds, with the price stepping down in fixed increments. When the timer ends, Moto clears the price by selecting a driver at the lowest price level that still has at least one eligible driver available, using clear tie-break rules when multiple drivers qualify at that same price.

Q: Is Auto bidding? A: No. Auto is not bidding. It is a calm, time-based price-clearing process.

Q: Why does the Auto price drop? A: Auto uses a step-down timer. The price drops in fixed increments during the 30–45 second window.

Q: What happens when the Auto timer ends? A: Moto selects a driver at the lowest price level that still has at least one eligible driver available, using tie-break rules if needed.

Q: If no eligible drivers exist at the lowest price, what happens? A: Moto picks the lowest price level that still has at least one eligible driver available.

## Common Confusions

Q: Is Auto the same as Flex? A: No. Flex is negotiation. Auto is a time-based price-clearing process.

Q: Is Auto a competition between drivers? A: No. Auto is not bidding and is not framed as a win/lose game.

Q: Does the rider "win" Auto? A: No. Auto is not a game. It is a timed price-clearing process.

Q: Which mode should be used if speed matters most? A: Instant is designed for speed.

Q: Which mode should be used if the rider wants control over price? A: Flex is negotiation and gives price control.

Q: Which mode should be used if the rider wants a calm automatic process? A: Auto is the calm, time-based price-clearing option.
