# Rider Customization

module: customization

## Overview

Q: What customization options exist? A: Conversation preference, music/sound preference, driver notes, ordering for someone else, and trip sharing.

## Conversation Preferences

Q: What are the conversation options? A: No Preference, Quiet Mode, Chat Mode.

## Music and Sound

Q: What music/sound options exist? A: Quiet Mode, Radio/Music On.

## Notes

Q: Can I add special instructions for the driver? A: Yes. Notes for the driver are supported.

Q: Can I save notes so they show automatically next time? A: Notes can be saved and reused. Auto-apply behavior is not documented in the current Moto KB v1.

## Order for Someone Else

Q: Can I request a ride for someone else? A: Yes. Moto supports ordering a ride for someone else.

## Trip Sharing

Q: Can I share my trip with family or friends? A: Yes. Trip/location sharing is supported.
